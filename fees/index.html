What your fees pay for

Most of what you need to train is already included.

  • Tuition in small classes, with a low student-to-instructor ratio
  • Your own welding machine and booth for practice
  • Breakfast and lunch on every training day
  • Safety glasses (UGX 5,000)
  • Your welding materials: electrodes and steel for practice
  • Your certificate when you finish

Don't buy anything before your interview. We'll tell you exactly what to get and where to get it.

How and when you pay

Pay registration when you register, and each semester's fee at the start of that semester. We accept mobile money, bank transfer and cash. If you need to pay in instalments, ask us at your interview.

Subsidised

Why it costs so little

Training a welder costs more than you pay. Followers of The Way, the NGO behind Kazo Technical School, pays part of every student's tuition.

That's how the semester fee stays at UGX 800,000 and still includes your meals and welding materials. Because every student already gets this support, we don't offer separate scholarships at the moment.

  • Part of every student's tuition is paid by our NGO
  • Meals on training days are included
  • Welding materials are included

Still want to see the place first?

Visit on a Saturday morning. It is free, it takes two hours, and you can ask about fees face to face.
